# Code of Conduct

AI For Life is a friendly student club. This repo follows the same rules as our meetings.

- Be respectful and welcoming. Beginners are the point.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, or content that targets or embarrasses anyone.
- Never post private information about yourself or others (passwords, keys, client or health details).
- Credit other people's work and link your sources.

If something bothers you, tell the maintainer (open an issue or message them in the club chat). Content that breaks these rules may be removed.
