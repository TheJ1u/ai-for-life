AI For Life Club

Learning AI together, one step at a time.

AI For Life is a club for people who want to understand AI and actually use it in school, work and everyday life. We share what we are trying, swap tips, and help each other get started. No experience needed.

What we do

Learn together

We talk through new AI tools and ideas in plain language, so nobody has to figure it out alone.

Try things out

We test tools on real projects, then share what worked, what did not, and the prompts behind it.

Share what we find

Good finds go in the club chat and on this site, so everyone can use them and add their own.

This site is our shared notebook: guides to the tools we have covered, a glossary of the words you will hear, and a roadmap for getting started.

How to use this site

1. Pick a tool

Each page explains what it is, why it matters, and gives you steps to start today.

2. Copy a prompt

Every guide has a starter prompt or code snippet you can copy in one click.

3. Learn the words

The glossary covers terms like API, CLI, MCP and the C-suite titles (CEO, CIO, CCO).
