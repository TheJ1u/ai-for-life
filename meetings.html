Why keep notes

Meeting notes are standard practice in business. They give anyone who missed a week a quick way to catch up, and over a semester they become a record of what the club has learned. Writing one entry is also an easy first contribution.

How to add a meeting entry

  1. Open meetings.html on GitHub and click the pencil icon.
  2. Find the line that says Add new meetings ABOVE this line.
  3. Paste the template below just above it and fill it in.
  4. Click Commit changes, then Open pull request.
