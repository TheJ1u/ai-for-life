Turn a script into shots

Works in: Any chat AI

Here is my 30-second script: [paste script]. Break it into 6 to 8 shots. For each shot give: a one-sentence visual description, camera movement, and a text-to-video prompt I can paste into an AI video tool.
Learning

Explain it like a business owner

Works in: Any chat AI

Explain [concept] to me like I run a small business and have never written code. Start with a simple business example, then add the technical detail, then give me one practical thing to try today.
Learning

Quiz me

Works in: Any chat AI

Quiz me on [topic] one question at a time. Start easy and get harder. After each answer, tell me if I was right, explain why, then ask the next question. Stop after 10 questions and summarize what I should review.
Writing

Clean up my dictation

Works in: Eloquent or any chat AI

Here is a rough voice transcript: [paste]. Rewrite it as clear text. Keep my meaning and my voice, remove filler words, fix grammar, and do not add facts I did not say.
Writing

Make it shorter

Business

Compare two tools

Works in: Any chat AI with web search

Compare [tool A] and [tool B] for [my goal]. Use a table with price, ease of use, best for, and limits. Check the official websites for current pricing and tell me which claims you could not verify.

Find the cheapest way

Why prompts matter for business

A reusable prompt is a small business process: write it once, and anyone on the team gets the same quality every time. Companies keep prompt libraries the same way they keep email templates and scripts.
