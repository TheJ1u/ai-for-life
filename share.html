Add it to your home screen

Installing puts an AI For Life icon on your phone or computer, and it opens full-screen like an app. It also keeps working with a weak connection.

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots).
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Computer (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Open the site.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or the menu, then Install AI For Life.

Tip for meetings

Put the QR code on a slide or print the page (Ctrl+P) and show it at the start of the meeting. Anyone can scan it and follow along.
