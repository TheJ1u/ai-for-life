Why a showcase

A project you can point to beats a line on a resume. Employers and investors look for evidence you can build, and a public page with your name on it is evidence. It also shows the rest of the club what is possible with free tools.

Add your project

  1. Open showcase.html on GitHub and click the pencil icon.
  2. Find the line that says Add new projects ABOVE this line and paste the template below just above it.
  3. Fill it in, click Commit changes, then Open pull request.
