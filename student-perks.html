Check before paying

Student plans and discounts

A free tier, a student benefit, a limited promotion and school-provided access are different things. Check the account’s final eligibility and billing screen before claiming anything.

Official sources checked October 9, 2026. We have not verified any BYU- or UVU-provided subscription. Benefits that could not be supported by official sources have been omitted.

Student benefit

GitHub Education and Copilot Student

Eligibility: Students aged 13+ enrolled in a degree or diploma program need a personal GitHub account and current enrollment evidence.

Included: GitHub Pro and Student Developer Pack partner offers; Copilot Student requires separate activation and has plan-specific limits.

Deadline and renewal: No general claim deadline listed. Copilot eligibility is reevaluated monthly; education and partner benefits have their own expiry and reverification rules. Do not assume a partner trial renews free. If activation shows only paid plans, do not buy expecting reimbursement.

Limited promotion

Google AI student offer

Eligibility: Age 18+, eligible higher-education institution and country, SheerID verification, personal Google account and qualifying payment method. School Workspace accounts, family-group members and certain existing subscriptions are excluded.

Included: 12 months of the eligible Google AI plan at no charge. The included plan depends on the institution’s country; confirm your exact plan and limits in the offer flow.

Deadline and renewal: Claim by December 31, 2026. Automatically renews at the applicable standard monthly price unless cancelled before the offer ends. Limits may differ from paid subscriptions.

Student benefit

Notion Education

Eligibility: Students or teachers at recognized accredited higher-education institutions using their school email as their primary Notion email. Individual workspaces must have one member.

Included: Education plan with unlimited uploads, 30-day page history and up to 100 guests. Student-organization workspaces have a separate application. No paid AI entitlement is promised here.

Deadline and renewal: No promotional claim deadline listed. Verify the school email annually; otherwise the workspace moves to the Free plan.

School-provided access / education benefit

Microsoft Office 365 Education

Eligibility: Students and educators with an eligible school email at a qualifying institution. Verify with Microsoft and campus IT.

Included: Eligible Office 365 A1 access. Do not infer desktop Office or a paid Copilot subscription from A1 eligibility.

Deadline and renewal: No promotional claim deadline listed. Eligibility can be rechecked at any time and access depends on continued enrollment or employment at a qualifying institution. Save your files before eligibility ends.

School-provided access

Claude Education

Eligibility: Institutional plan arranged by a university; an .edu address alone does not establish entitlement.

Included: Institution-wide Claude access where the institution has arranged it. BYU and UVU access have not been confirmed. Personal Claude Free and paid plans are separate.

Deadline and renewal: No universal student claim deadline or renewal term verified. Ask campus IT about provisioning, included features and the institution’s contract. No individual paid student benefit is claimed.

Limited promotion

OpenAI ChatGPT Back to School

Eligibility: Current full-time or part-time college or graduate students at eligible U.S. degree-granting institutions, verified by SheerID. New users and directly billed monthly Plus subscribers may qualify; store-billed subscriptions are excluded while store-billed.

Included: Four free monthly billing periods of ChatGPT Plus, including ChatGPT Work and other Plus features. A valid payment method is required.

Deadline and renewal: Claim once by October 31, 2026. Renews at $20/month afterward unless cancelled. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current monthly billing period and forfeits any remaining promotional months.

Free tiers are separate

Personal free options do not require a student promotion. Start with the text exercises in our ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini guides and check their linked plan pages for limits. A free account does not include every paid feature.

Before you claim

  1. Check the official link and your own eligibility.
  2. Read the renewal amount, cancellation timing and storage limits.
  3. Record the expiry date and set your own reminder.
  4. Check campus IT separately for institutional access. Never send passwords or verification documents to the club.
