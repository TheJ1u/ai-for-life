Quick demo

What it is

CapCut is a video editor for phone, desktop and web. AI generators give you raw clips; CapCut is where you trim them, add music, captions, voiceover and transitions, and export in the right shape (such as vertical 9:16 for Reels and TikTok).

Why people use it

  • Most AI clips are short. Editing is how you turn several clips into one story.
  • Auto-captions and templates make professional-looking videos quickly.
  • A free tier covers most beginner needs (some features are paid).

How to get started

  1. Install CapCut (desktop is easiest) or use the web editor at capcut.com.
  2. Create a project and set the ratio: 9:16 for Reels/TikTok/Shorts, 16:9 for YouTube.
  3. Import your clips from Higgsfield or FLUX and drop them on the timeline.
  4. Trim each clip, add music or voiceover, then run auto-captions.
  5. Export, and keep your project file so you can re-edit later.

Full walkthrough: turn raw clips into a finished video

Menus differ between phone, desktop and web, but the process is the same. Check capcut.com for what is free right now.

1. Know your finish line

Pick the platform first, because it sets the shape: 9:16 vertical for TikTok, Reels and Shorts; 16:9 wide for YouTube and websites; 1:1 square for some feeds. Pick a length too, such as 15 or 30 seconds.

2. Learn the five words

  • Timeline: the strip at the bottom where clips sit in order.
  • Track: a row on the timeline (video, music, text).
  • Split: cut a clip into two pieces at the playhead.
  • Trim: shorten a clip by dragging its edge.
  • Export: render the finished video into a file.

3. Build the video

  1. Create a new project and set the aspect ratio.
  2. Import your clips and drag them onto the timeline in story order: hook, middle, call to action.
  3. Trim the start and end of each clip. Cut every second that does not help. The first 2 seconds must grab attention.
  4. Add music or a voiceover on a separate track. Lower the music so speech is clear.
  5. Run auto-captions, then read every line and fix mistakes. Captions help people who watch with the sound off.
  6. Add your logo and a final text card with the call to action ("Call today", "Follow for more").

4. Keep text in the safe area

On vertical video, apps cover the top and bottom edges with buttons and captions. Keep important text in the middle of the frame.

5. Export

Choose 1080p resolution and the frame rate that matches your clips (often 30 fps). Export the file, then also save the project so you can edit later.

6. Final check before you post

  • Watch the whole video with sound and again muted.
  • Do you have the right to use the music, images and clips?
  • Are names, prices and claims correct?
  • Does it say AI-generated where required?

7. Repurpose a long video

Import a 30-minute recording, find 3 to 5 strong 20-second moments, split them out, add captions and a title to each, and export them as separate clips. One recording becomes a week of posts.

Common problems

  • Captions are wrong: edit the text by hand. Names and brand terms are the usual errors.
  • Video is blurry after export: raise the export resolution, and avoid zooming in too far on low-quality clips.
  • Export has a watermark or is blocked: that feature may be paid. Choose a free option or check the plan.
  • Audio out of sync: re-import the clip or export again at the same frame rate as the source.

Business use case

Scenario: A consultant records a 30-minute webinar and wants a week of short posts from it.

How it works: Import the recording, cut the best moments, add auto-captions, resize for vertical video, and export. Add AI-generated clips as B-roll where needed.

Measure success by: Number of posts produced per recording, time per clip, and views and follows from the posts.

Watch out for: Check the app's current free features and export limits. Use only music and images you have the rights to, and check captions for mistakes.

Quick facts

  • Free and paid tiers; features and prices change, so check capcut.com.
  • Pairs well with any AI video tool because you import standard video files.
  • Check licenses for music and effects before using in ads.
