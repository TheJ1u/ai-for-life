Writing and productivity

ChatGPT for beginners

Draft a customer email using fictional information, then check every claim before using the result.

What it is

ChatGPT is a conversational AI assistant. You describe a task in ordinary language and refine its answer with follow-up instructions. An answer can sound convincing while being wrong.

Why people use it

It can help organize information and produce a first draft. You remain responsible for the final work.

How to get started

  1. Open ChatGPT official app and follow its account and age requirements.
  2. Start a new conversation. Review privacy settings before entering information.
  3. Paste the practice prompt below. No paid feature or upload is needed for this text exercise.

Try this: Draft a customer email

Write a friendly email under 100 words using only these fictional facts: our shop received a request for a blue notebook; stock has not been checked; a team member will check tomorrow. Do not promise availability, delivery, or a discount. End with one clear next step. List any missing information separately.

Full walkthrough

1. Prepare a safe example

Use the invented facts above. Do not paste customer records, school records, account numbers, or private company information.

2. Make and revise a draft

  1. Send the prompt and read the entire response.
  2. Ask: “Which statements came directly from my input, and which are assumptions?”
  3. Ask it to correct any unsupported statement and shorten the output.
  4. Copy the reviewed version into your own document. Keep the original facts nearby.

3. Check your work

The draft must not claim the notebook is in stock. Check its promises and word count against your instructions. Recheck arithmetic with a calculator. Open original sources for factual claims; a citation or another AI answer is not proof.

Common problems

  • Invented details: ask for placeholders and remove unsupported claims yourself.
  • Usage limit: save your prompt and try again when access resets. Paying is optional.
  • A missing feature: use the text prompt directly; menus differ by account and device.

Business use case

Scenario: A small shop needs a reviewed draft for routine work.

How it works: A staff member supplies approved facts, requests a draft, checks it and obtains the usual approval before use.

Measure success: Compare drafting plus review time with the manual process, and count corrections. Any subscription must save more staff time than it costs.

Watch out: Do not automate sending. Follow employer privacy rules and applicable rules for regulated customer information.

Free and paid

A free option and paid plans are available. Limits and included tools vary; this exercise uses basic text chat. School-provided access is separate and has not been confirmed for BYU or UVU.
